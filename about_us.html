Meet Kris, Founder of Delva Web Design

I'm the owner and lead developer behind Delva Web Design, based right here in Newark, Delaware.

How It All Started

My journey into web design began shortly after high school, when I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript just to build little portfolio sites for fun. What started as a hobby quickly turned into a passion, and eventually, a purpose.

For the past 5 years, I've worked as a front-end developer in the automotive industry, where I sharpened my skills and learned the ins and outs of web design, digital marketing, and SEO from the inside out. But somewhere along the way, I realized I wanted more than just a career. I wanted a life that let me be present. I wanted to build something of my own so I could stay home and watch my daughter grow up, not miss the little moments while commuting to an office.

Why Delva Web Design Exists

My wife runs her own small business, and watching her navigate the challenges of getting online, standing out, and connecting with customers gave me a front-row seat to what small business owners really need. I saw firsthand how overwhelming web design, marketing, and SEO can feel when it's not your area of expertise, and I wanted to change that.

That's why I started Delva Web Design: to give back to the small business community by making professional, high-quality websites accessible to business owners who aren't necessarily tech-savvy but deserve a strong online presence just the same.

What We Do

At Delva Web Design, I offer custom website packages designed specifically for small businesses. Whether you're just getting started or ready for a refresh, my goal is to make the entire process simple, transparent, and stress free, with no confusing jargon and no guesswork.

Every Site I Build Is

  • Fast: Built for performance, so your visitors never wait
  • Secure: Protecting your business and your customers' trust
  • Custom Made: Tailored to your brand, not a generic template
  • Fully Responsive: Looking great on everything from desktop monitors to mobile phones
